### Short Description
VibeLab is an AI-powered learning and portfolio platform for non-technical creators, freelancers, and small businesses. It helps users turn ideas into content kits, AI-generated videos, live websites, resumes, and job-ready portfolios through a structured six-week program.

### Problem
Millions of non-technical professionals and small business owners in the MENA region want to use AI, but most AI courses stop at tool tutorials. Learners still lack a clear path from learning to marketable output: a live website, a content portfolio, a resume, and a repeatable client acquisition workflow.

### Solution
VibeLab combines AI education, project-based learning, portfolio building, resume analysis, and job-finding workflows in one platform. Users complete assessments, build live projects, receive AI feedback, create public profiles, and follow guided market paths for website production, content creation, freelancing, and MVP building.

### Target Market
Our first market is Persian-speaking creators, freelancers, students, and small businesses, followed by UAE/MENA SMEs and non-technical professionals who need practical AI upskilling with visible business outputs.

### Business Model
- Six-week cohort program
- Corporate AI upskilling workshops
- Subscription for portfolio/job-finder tools
- Marketplace/referral fees for project opportunities
- Premium mentoring and project review

### Traction
- Working product deployed on Cloudflare Workers
- Cloudflare D1 database connected
- User registration, login, assessment, dashboard, admin dashboard, resume kit and job-finder modules are live
- Initial market positioning for Iran and global freelance markets implemented

### Why UAE
The UAE is investing heavily in practical AI adoption, public-sector AI transformation, startup acceleration, and SME digitization. VibeLab can support the UAE/MENA market by turning AI literacy into measurable business outputs: content, websites, MVPs, and job-ready portfolios.
